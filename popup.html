A little clarity.

Select text in a ChatGPT response, then choose Explain this or Ask…. Your answer appears in a compact floating panel.

  1. Sign in to chatgpt.com in Chrome.
  2. Refresh existing chats after installing.
  3. Select text to explain, translate, or ask.
Uses your signed-in web account. No API key. A separate helper tab submits each question you start. Normal usage limits apply, and separate chats may appear in your history.

The interface is in English. Answers follow the language of the surrounding ChatGPT response, or a language you explicitly request.

Use − to minimize, or Esc / × to close the panel and its helper tab. Closing does not delete ChatGPT history. For sign-in or verification prompts, choose Open helper tab, resolve the prompt, then retry.
